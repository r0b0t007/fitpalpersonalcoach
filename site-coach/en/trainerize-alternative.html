Trainerize alternative: how FitPal Coach compares

Updated 6 November 2026.

Trainerize is one of the most used coaching platforms. FitPal Coach is younger and narrower: it is built for an independent coach who finds clients on Instagram. This comparison sticks to published facts. Trainerize's prices were taken from its pricing page on 6 November 2026, in US dollars; check them there before you decide, as they change.

Prices

Free
Trainerize: Basic, 1 client, no time limit. A 30-day trial with unlimited clients, add-ons not included. FitPal Coach: Free, 3 clients, no time limit, no card.
Entry plan
Trainerize: Grow, $9 a month for 2 clients ($10 in the same page's FAQ). FitPal Coach: Solo, €19 a month including VAT, for 10 clients.
Next plan
Trainerize: Pro, from $23 a month for 5 clients ($25 in the FAQ), in tiers up to 200 clients. FitPal Coach: Pro, €39 for 30 clients; Studio, €79 for 75.
Paid add-ons
Trainerize: advanced nutrition $20–45 a month, Business $25, video coaching $10, Stripe payments $10, custom branded app $169 one-off. FitPal Coach: none; every plan has everything.
Currency and tax
Trainerize: US dollars. FitPal Coach: euros, VAT included.

The differences that matter

How a follower becomes a client

FitPal Coach starts at the link in your bio: a page with your name, an "Ask to train with me" button, a code by email, and your answer. It works inside Instagram's browser. With a starter program set, workout 1 is ready the day you accept.

Money

Trainerize can take your clients' payments through its Stripe add-on. FitPal Coach stays out of payments: your clients pay you as they do today, by transfer, cash or your own payment link.

Languages

The FitPal Coach app runs in English, French, Spanish and German, chosen by each coach and each client.

When Trainerize will suit you better

  • You want clients to pay inside the app, by card.
  • You want an app under your own brand in the App Store and Google Play.
  • You run video coaching, or a studio with several coaches.
  • You coach more than 75 clients, the limit of FitPal's largest plan.

If you work alone, your clients come from Instagram and pay you your own way, FitPal Coach costs less for more clients, with nothing to add on. The Free plan lets you try it with three real clients first. During launch, paid plans are switched on by request: contact@fitpal.ma. See the pricing.

Questions

Can I import my programs from Trainerize?

There is no automatic import. Send your programs as a PDF, a spreadsheet or screenshots and during launch, we enter them for you at no cost.

Are these Trainerize prices current?

They were taken on 6 November 2026 from Trainerize's pricing page, which itself shows two different prices for some plans. Check their page before you decide.
