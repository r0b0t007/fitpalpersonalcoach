Who is responsible

For their clients' data (programs, sessions, measurements, check-ins, the answers to a request to join), the coach is the data controller: they decide to collect it and what it is for. FitPal Coach, published by Ahmed Chioua, is the processor: it hosts and processes that data on the coach's behalf, on their instructions, and for nothing else. For the coach's own account (identity, email address, subscription), FitPal Coach is the controller.

Data is never sold or used for advertising, and it exports to JSON on request at any time.

Asking to train with a coach

A coach's page lets you ask to train with them. The form collects a first name, an email address (checked with a code), a goal, a level, a number of sessions a week and the equipment you have; and, only if you want to give them, your injuries or pain and a WhatsApp number.

Injuries and pain are health data (GDPR Article 9; Moroccan law 09-08). They are processed only with your explicit consent, given with a separate checkbox, and only to prepare your program. Only the coach you write to can read them. Legal basis: your explicit consent (GDPR Articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)). Your account and the coaching itself rest on providing the service (Article 6(1)(b)).

  • A request whose address is not checked is deleted within 24 hours.
  • A request the coach does not answer lapses after 14 days.
  • A declined or lapsed request: your answers (injuries and number included) are erased at once; your first name and address are deleted 30 days later.
  • An accepted request is kept while you train with that coach, and deleted as soon as that ends: the coach removes you from their clients, you move to another coach, or the coach leaves FitPal Coach.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by asking the coach, who can delete your answers from the app, or contact@fitpal.ma; your request and your answers are then deleted.

Minimum age

Asking to train with a coach from their page is for people aged 16 or older: the form asks you to confirm it. A coach who invites a client under 16 themselves does so under their own responsibility as controller, and must have the agreement of a parent or guardian.

Security log

Actions on accounts (sign-ins, invitations, the coach's decisions, settings) are recorded with the id and name of the accounts concerned (and, for a refused sign-up, the name typed in), without IP addresses, and kept for at most 90 days, to protect the service from abuse and trace the cause of an incident. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

Right to object. You can object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to this processing based on legitimate interest (GDPR Article 21), by writing to contact@fitpal.ma.

Signing in

Sign-in uses a passkey (WebAuthn) or a code or link sent by email: no password is ever stored.
