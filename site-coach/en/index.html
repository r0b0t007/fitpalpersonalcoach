01How it works

Three steps, and your follower is training.

  1. 1

    The link

    Your page: a photo, your name, a few lines about your coaching and, if you like, your WhatsApp. Its address goes in your Instagram bio.

  2. 2

    The request

    The follower gives their goal, their level, their days, any injuries and their equipment, then confirms their email with a code. You get a notification and an email, and you accept with one tap.

  3. 3

    Workout 1

    The starter program you chose for your page is waiting the moment you accept. They train the same day, and every workout they finish shows on their profile.

05Questions

Frequently asked questions

Where does the money go?

To you. Your clients pay you directly, by bank transfer, in cash or however you do it today. FitPal never touches those payments and takes no commission.

Do my clients have to install an app?

No. They open your link and train in the browser. They can add the app to their home screen in two taps; on an iPhone, that is what lets them get reminders.

Does it work from Instagram?

Yes. Your follower sends their request without leaving Instagram’s browser: they get a six-digit code by email and type it on the same page, and the answer reaches them there and by email. The page also offers to open in Safari or Chrome, to add the app to their home screen; that is a suggestion, not a required step.

What about my data and my clients’ data?

It is hosted by our providers: the app and its database at Namecheap, in the United States, and the nightly backups encrypted and stored in the European Union. It is never sold, and you can export it at any time. In the app, the health answers from the intake are visible only to the client’s coach. The full list is on the legal page.

What happens after 3 clients?

You move to Solo to accept up to 10. Until then, new requests keep coming in and wait for your answer.
