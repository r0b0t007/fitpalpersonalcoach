02The product

Two apps, one account

You work in the coach dashboard. Your clients get the training app. Every screenshot below comes from the product as it runs, not from a mockup.

The morning dashboard

One page that answers the only question that matters before your first client: who needs you today.

  • Who is training right now, live
  • Who has done nothing for seven days
  • Who is waiting on a reply from you

Your roster on one page

Every client's 28-day adherence, colour before number. The one slipping shows up before they leave.

  • 28-day adherence, client by client
  • Green dot: in a session right now
  • Program sent, accepted, or left unanswered

What your client sees

A real app, under your name, installed on the home screen. Not a PDF, not a link to a spreadsheet.

  • Today's session, and the week ahead
  • Their weight and curve, against their goal
  • Works offline, syncs afterwards

The numbers, when you need them

Adherence against the plan, not a flattering average. Enough to hold a hard conversation with facts in it.

  • Adherence measured against scheduled days
  • Estimated 1RM per exercise
  • Bodyweight, measurements, muscle balance

05Questions

What people ask

I only have eight clients. Is that too few?

No. This is built for one person with a phone, not for a gym with a front desk. Eight clients is where the time saved shows up most, because there is nobody to hand the admin to.

Do my clients have to download an app?

They open a link and add it to their home screen. No store, no account to create, no password. Native Android and iOS builds exist if you would rather use those.

What happens if I stop?

You leave with your data, exported as JSON. The instance is yours, not ours.

The coach dashboard is English only. Why?

Because the client app was translated first, and announcing a translation that does not exist would be dishonest. The four languages are what your clients see. Translating the dashboard is planned.

What does it cost?

Priced on the call, based on client count and hosting. There is no per-client subscription: your bill does not grow because your business is working.
