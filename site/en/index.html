Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to launch my gym's app?

About a week: branding, domain, class timetable, then your first invitation codes.

Do my members need the App Store?

No — the app installs from the browser (PWA) on iPhone and Android in one tap, with no app store review.

Who owns the data?

You do. Each gym runs its own dedicated instance, exportable at any time; member data is never sold or shared.

How much does it cost?

A one-time setup quoted per club (branding, domain, configuration, training), then a flat monthly hosting fee with unlimited members — no per-member subscription.

What if I stop?

You leave with your data — and since the core is open source (openGym, AGPL), any other provider can take over your instance.
