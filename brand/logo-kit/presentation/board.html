What we heard

The brief

Software for independent coaches in Morocco with 5 to 40 clients. It replaces WhatsApp DMs, voice notes and the spreadsheet: clients get a real training app, and the coach sees who is dropping off. The mark has to work as an app icon on black, as a favicon, and in the brand's gold on warm graphite, without leaning on the fitness clichés (dumbbells, bolts, flames, flexed arms).

dependabledirectattentivedisciplinedpremium

Ownable in a category of dumbbells and lightning bolts, readable at 16 px, works in one colour, sits in the existing gold-on-graphite brand.

Final design

One Spine

An f and a p on one stem: coach and client on the same backbone.

  • The f's crossbar runs straight into the p's bowl, so the two letters read as one piece
  • No dumbbell, no bolt: a letterform, so it stays ownable in a cliché-heavy category
  • A heavier small-size drawing keeps it legible as a 16 px favicon
  • Gold on warm graphite, the palette the site and the app already use
