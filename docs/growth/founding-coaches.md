# Founding Coaches: who, the deal, and the one-page agreement

Written 2026-11-06 for CHW-349 (growth plan §4, weeks 5–8). Ten coaches who already train
clients on FitPal Coach get Pro free for as long as their coach contract runs (§2). In return they give a short video, one story and
permission to quote them. Until the first agreement is signed, the messaging doc's §7 still
holds: no testimonials, no "X coaches already", no success stories.

Not legal advice. The agreement was checked against the contract-review checklist in
lawve-ai/awesome-legal-skills (contract-review). The disclosure rules come from the sources in §6.
The points marked "to verify" are for the owner.

## 1. Who

- **Ten coaches:** at least 4 in Morocco, 3 in France or Belgium and 3 in Spain. Belgian coaches
  only if they post in French. A Dutch-speaking coach would need a Dutch agreement and a Dutch
  label (§4), and neither is written yet.
- **Already on FitPal Coach, with 3 or more clients training:** 3+ clients with a workout in the
  last 7 days. The admin panel's coach seats list shows who has 3+ active clients. Then confirm
  the 7 days with the coach, on a call where they show their Clients screen. The funnel's
  "habit" stage can't be used: it counts only self-serve coaches 30+ days in, as weekly totals
  without names. Their clients' week shows what the product does. A coach who isn't using it
  would be endorsing something they haven't tried.
- **Only coaches who use the app.** Nobody is recruited for this by a cold DM. The invitation
  goes to coaches already in a conversation with the founder, for example over WhatsApp after
  the setup.
- **Not chosen for their follower count alone.** A coach with 8k followers and 15 clients
  training is worth more here than one with 90k and 3 clients.

## 2. The deal

**What the coach gets:**
- **Pro (30 clients) free, for as long as their coach contract runs.** Granted from the admin
  panel (`admin.licence.grant`, plan `pro`). It's a `manual` licence with no end date. The coach
  terms (`conditions.html` §8) still apply: FitPal can end any coach's contract on 30 days'
  notice, or at once for a serious breach. The agreement adds a promise: we won't end their
  contract, or change the plans, in order to take Pro back. That is what "for life" means here,
  and the agreement says it in those words.
- **Granted once the video and the story are delivered,** not at signature. From then on it is
  theirs.
- **Pro stays even if they later withdraw their permission (§3).** Their permission is only
  worth something if they can take it back without losing anything, so Pro doesn't depend on
  it.
- **If they pay already:** a grant is refused while a Creem subscription can still bill
  (`Accounts::grant`). A cancel the coach makes in Creem's portal takes effect only at the end
  of the paid period (`scheduled_cancel`), and until then it still counts as billable. So the
  founder cancels it at once from Creem's dashboard, then grants Pro. Whether to refund the
  unused part is the owner's call. Billing is off today, so this can't happen yet.

**What the coach gives:**
1. **One video, 30–60 seconds,** filmed on their phone, about their own experience with
   FitPal Coach, in their own words. We may suggest topics, never a script.
2. **One story** on their account mentioning @fitpal.ma, with their own FitPal link. It shows
   the label (§4) for as long as it is up.
3. **Permission** to show the video, quotes from it, their first name, handle and profile photo
   on fitpal.ma and on @fitpal.ma.
4. **Optionally,** co-hosting one of the two Instagram Lives (§5).

**What the video and quotes can't contain:**
- **A client's face, name, messages or numbers,** unless that client has given the coach written
  permission first. A coached client's weight and check-ins are health data.
- **Results promised to followers** ("tu perdras 10 kg"), before/after bodies, or numbers we
  can't stand behind (messaging §6).
- **A competitor named,** or anything about one we haven't checked (messaging §4).

**What we promise in return:**
- **We publish what they said.** We may shorten it, add subtitles and the label, but never
  change its meaning. They see the final version before it goes out, and nothing goes out
  without their OK.
- **Every testimonial says what they got.** On fitpal.ma and @fitpal.ma it carries the line
  « Founding Coach — Pro offert par FitPal Coach » / «Founding Coach — Pro gratis de FitPal
  Coach». A reader has to know the endorsement came with something in return.
- **No paid ads with their face** unless they reply « Lu et accepté, publicités comprises » (§3). The
  retargeting in week 9 (CHW-354) uses only what they ticked.

## 3. The agreement

One page, sent as a PDF or in the body of an e-mail. It is accepted by replying from the
coach's account e-mail with one of two sentences, so the ads choice is written down without a
box to tick:
- **No ads:** « Lu et accepté » / «Leído y aceptado».
- **With ads:** « Lu et accepté, publicités comprises » / «Leído y aceptado, anuncios incluidos».
Fill in the brackets:
- `[prénom nom]` and `[@handle]`: the coach;
- `[e-mail]`: the address on their FitPal Coach account;
- `[nom]` and `[adresse]`: the publisher's, exactly as on fitpal.ma's mentions légales;
- `[date]`: the day it is sent.

**Where the signed copies go:** the acceptance replies stay in the founder's mailbox. A private
sheet, never this repo, lists for each coach:
- handle, the date sent, and ads yes or no;
- the date the licence was granted;
- the end date: 3 years after the date sent. Put it in the founder's calendar too.
- the date of any withdrawal.

**Before the first testimonial goes on fitpal.ma,** it needs a home outside the public repo, so a
withdrawal can really delete it: for example an R2 bucket (the backups already use R2) served
under fitpal.ma, which the page reads at load. That is a small build of its own. Until it
exists, testimonials go on @fitpal.ma only, where a post can be deleted.

**When it ends,** by withdrawal or at the end date:
1. **Within 7 days:** take the video and quotes off fitpal.ma and off @fitpal.ma, and stop any
   ad that uses them.
2. **Within 30 days:** delete every copy, wherever it is:
   - the video file in the e-mail or WhatsApp thread it came in;
   - the founder's drive;
   - the site's git history can't be cleaned. fitpal.ma is built from this public repo
     (`site-coach/`), so anything committed there stays in its history even after it is
     removed, and that includes a quote, a handle or a photo, not only a video.
3. **What stays:** the acceptance reply (with any attachment deleted from it) and the end
   date, as point 7 says. The sheet row keeps only those.

## 4. The label on each platform

The coach's story is a promotion made in exchange for something, so it has to say so on screen,
for as long as it is up:

| Coach's country | Label | Where |
|---|---|---|
| France, Belgium (posting in French), Morocco | « Collaboration commerciale » (or « Publicité ») | written on the story, readable, the whole time |
| Spain | «Publi», «Publicidad» or «En colaboración con FitPal Coach» | the same |

Instagram's own "Paid partnership" tag can go on top, but doesn't replace the written label.
@fitpal.ma's own posts of a testimonial are FitPal's ads, so they are labelled as ours, with
the "Founding Coach — Pro offert" line.

## 5. The two Lives

- **Topic:** the business of online coaching, not FitPal. How they set their price, how they get
  paid, how they find clients on Instagram. FitPal comes up only when the guest brings it up.
- **Format:** 30–40 minutes, @fitpal.ma hosting and the Founding Coach as guest. One in French,
  one in Spanish.
- **At the start,** the host says the guest is a Founding Coach who uses FitPal Coach with Pro
  for free. The Live is FitPal's content, and the relationship is said out loud.
- **Questions sent in:** answered without names on screen.

## 6. Sources, and what to verify

- **France:** loi n° 2023-451 du 9 juin 2023, art. 5. It requires « Publicité » or
  « Collaboration commerciale », clear and visible for the whole promotion
  ([economie.gouv.fr](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/node/3222759)). **To verify:** art. 5 was
  rewritten by ordonnance n° 2024-978 and given a décret, n° 2026-233 of 30 March 2026. Read the
  consolidated text on Légifrance before the first story. Also check whether a free service
  counts as consideration under its art. 1. We label in any case.
- **Spain:** the influencer code of conduct (AEA and Autocontrol, in force since January 2021)
  counts giving a product or service for free as consideration. It asks for "publicidad",
  "publi" or "en colaboración con" where the advertising isn't obvious
  ([ineaf.es](https://www.ineaf.es/tribuna/codigo-de-conducta-publicidad-con-influencers/),
  [genbeta.com](https://www.genbeta.com/redes-sociales-y-comunidades/publicidad-influencers-tiene-regulacion-codigo-obligara-a-senalar-anuncios-regalos-se-consideraran-pagos/amp)).
  Consumo has warned of fines over €100,000 for covert advertising
  ([reasonwhy.es](https://www.reasonwhy.es/actualidad/consumo-advierte-grandes-influencers-posibilidad-sanciones-publicidad-encubierta)).
  **To verify:** the current code and the Ley General de Comunicación Audiovisual. This page's
  sources date from 2016 to 2023.
- **Belgium and Morocco:** no specific rule was checked. EU unfair-practices law (covert
  advertising) applies in Belgium, and Morocco's loi 31-08 covers misleading advertising. We use
  the French label in both. **To verify** before the first Belgian or Moroccan story.
- **The agreement itself:**
  - **No choice of law or court is written in.** The coaches are professionals in three
    countries, and the owner should decide whether to add one.
  - **The 3-year limit and the 7- and 30-day removal times** are our own choices, not legal
    requirements. Check they are acceptable.
  - **Accepting by e-mail reply,** instead of a signed PDF, is a choice made for simplicity.
    Check it is enough proof.
