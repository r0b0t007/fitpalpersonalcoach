# Done for you within 24 hours: setting up a coach's program (CHW-348)

What the founder does when a coach takes the offer in the DMs (`2026-11-dm-outreach.md`, the reply
to a yes) or on fitpal.ma (messaging §3, proof 4): the coach sends their program on WhatsApp, and
within 24 hours it is in their FitPal Coach account, set as the starter on their page, with their
link back to them.

**The founder never signs in as the coach.** The coach signs in with a passkey or a sign-in link
sent to their own e-mail, and neither is ever asked for. The program is built in the founder's own coach
account and copied across from the admin panel (Users → the coach → **Copy a program to this
coach**). Everything that is the coach's to say — their handle, photo, bio, WhatsApp number, and
switching the page on — the coach does on their phone, in two minutes, from text the founder sends.

## 1. The rules

1. **We enter their program; we don't write it.** No new exercises, sets or advice of our own. If
   the PDF is unclear (a missing number of sets, an exercise we can't identify), we ask; we don't
   guess.
2. **Only their own program.** The coach confirms it is theirs to use. A program they bought from
   someone else, or a client's personal plan, is not entered.
3. **No client data.** We don't want their clients' names, photos or measurements. If a file has
   any, it is deleted at once, unread beyond what it took to notice, and the coach is told why.
4. **24 hours from the last file, or we say so.** The clock starts when everything in §2 has
   arrived. If a setup won't make it, the coach hears it before the 24 hours are up, with a time.
   If the queue goes past a day, the DMs stop offering it (messaging §7).
5. **The founder's copy is deleted** once the coach has theirs (§3, step 6): the program belongs in
   one account, the coach's.

## 2. What the coach sends

The coach sends these on WhatsApp after the reply to a yes:

- **their program:** a PDF, a Sheet, or photos of it. One program per setup; a second one waits
  for the next.
- **a square photo of their face**, for their page and its link preview;
- **two lines on what they do**, for the bio (at most 160 characters; the founder trims it);
- **the e-mail they signed up with**, to find their account in the admin panel;
- **a yes** to "is this program your own?" (rule 2), asked in the reply below.

Answer once everything has arrived, or ask for what is missing, in one message in the coach's
language (leave out the "still need" sentence when nothing is missing):

- **FR:** Merci ! Il me manque encore [ce qui manque] et je m'y mets. C'est bien ton programme
  à toi ? Et petite chose : envoie seulement ton programme, sans infos sur tes clients.
- **ES:** ¡Gracias! Me falta [lo que falta] y me pongo. ¿El programa es tuyo? Y una cosa: mándame
  solo tu programa, sin datos de tus clientes.
- **EN:** Thanks! I still need [what's missing] and then I'm on it. Is the program your own? And
  one thing: send only your program, nothing about your clients.

## 3. The setup, step by step

1. **Log the start.** In the Sheet's Setups tab (§5), add the coach's handle and the date and
   time the last file arrived.
2. **Find the account.** In the admin panel, Users, search for their e-mail. It must show
   `coach`, the name they gave, and a Created date that fits when they said they signed up. No
   match means they haven't finished signing up: tell them, and stop the clock.
   - **"(unconfirmed)"** means they haven't tapped the link in the mail we sent yet. An address
     nobody has confirmed proves nothing: anyone can type it at sign-up, and two accounts can
     carry the same one. If more than one row matches, or the name or date doesn't fit, ask them
     to tap the link first and search again; a confirmed address belongs to one account only.
3. **Build the program in your own account.** In the coach app (coach.fitpal.ma, signed in as
   yourself), open Programs → New program, and name it what the coach calls it. One routine per
   training day of their PDF, each exercise matched to the library. An exercise the library
   doesn't have is made from the exercise search ("Create your own exercise"), under the
   coach's name for it; it travels with the program. The limits are the save's: 20 routines,
   60 exercises a routine, 100 custom exercises.
   - Demo videos don't come across. A video you upload stays in your account; the coach adds
     their own from Account → Exercise demos.
   - A nutrition plan isn't entered. The coach sets daily targets when they send the program to
     a client.
4. **Check it against the PDF**, routine by routine: names, sets, reps, rest, the week's order.
5. **Copy it.** Admin panel → Users → the coach → ⋯ → **Copy a program to this coach** → pick
   the program → Submit. "Program copied" means it's in their library under a new id. The copy is
   theirs: nothing links it to yours, and editing either one changes nothing in the other. A
   refusal is shown as the app words it: not a coach, disabled, a plan that is read-only (grant
   one first), a program of that name already in their library (a second submit), or their
   50-program library full. A program missing from the list was saved
   before a newer rule; open it in your editor and save it again.
6. **Delete your copy** from your own Programs.
7. **Send the coach their part** (below), with their bio text ready to paste.
8. **Check the page.** When they say it's done, open `coach.fitpal.ma/c/<handle>` in a private
   window: their photo and bio show, and the page doesn't say they aren't taking requests. Log the time in the Setups
   tab. That is the end of the 24 hours.
9. **Delete their files** (§4).

## 4. The coach's files

The coach's PDF, Sheet, photos and bio are theirs. We keep them only for as long as the setup
takes:

- **Once the page is checked (§3, step 8):** delete their files from the WhatsApp chat ("Delete
  for me"), from the phone's gallery or Files, and from any computer they were opened on, Downloads
  and the trash included.
- **WhatsApp settings:** "Save to gallery" stays off on the phone used for setups, so files don't
  land in the camera roll or its cloud backup. Chat backups to iCloud or Google Drive can still
  hold a file that was in the chat; this is one more reason to delete it as soon as the setup is
  done. Set the chat backup to **Daily** (Settings › Chats › Chat backup), so a deleted file
  leaves the backup the next day, as the legal notice says; never "Off" for weeks then on again.
- **A setup that stalls:** files from a coach who stopped answering are deleted 14 days after
  their last message.
  Tell them, and ask them to send again if they come back.
- The chat itself, the coach's messages to us, stays as the record of what was asked and agreed,
  for as long as the coach's account exists. Delete it when the account closes, or sooner if the
  coach asks.
- The photo and bio the coach puts on their page are on their public page, by their choice. We
  keep no other copy.

## 5. Measuring it

The issue is done when this has been used on 5 coaches with a median under 24 hours.

**The Setups tab** goes in the same Google Sheet as the DM tracker. Its columns:

| Column | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| `handle` | the coach's Instagram handle |
| `files_in` | date and time the last file of §2 arrived |
| `page_open` | date and time the page was checked open (§3, step 8) |
| `hours` | `page_open` − `files_in`, in hours |
| `notes` | a few words: what slowed it down, what was asked |

- **Activation** (a client of theirs logs a workout) is read in the admin panel's coach funnel,
  by sign-up week, as the DM doc's Monday review reads it. It isn't tracked per coach here.
- **Monday review:** add the week's setups to the DM doc's Totals tab (setups done, median
  hours), then remove the `handle` and `notes` of every row older than 30 days. The `hours` stay,
  with no name.
- The admin panel's audit log has each copy (`admin.program.copy`, with the coach and the
  copy's id) and each page save (`coach.page.save`). Use it to check a time you forgot to
  write down. It is pruned after 90 days by default (`AUDIT_DAYS`).

## 6. What we don't do

- Design or change a program, or give training or nutrition advice.
- Sign in to a coach's account, or ask for their e-mail code or passkey.
- Choose their handle, publish their page, or write to their followers for them.
- Enter anything about their clients.
- Promise a time we can't keep: when we're late, the coach is told before the 24 hours run out.

## To verify (not legal advice)

- **Our role:** the coach's photo, bio and e-mail are the coach's own personal data. We process
  them as controller, to do the setup they asked for, under the coach terms (CHW-357). The program
  itself is their business content. Their clients' data is where we would be the coach's
  processor; rule 3 keeps it out of the setup altogether. The legal notice says we do this setup
  by hand over WhatsApp, and how long the files and the chat are kept (« Mise en place de votre
  programme », since 8 November 2026). Check that the basis (the coach's request, Art. 6(1)(b))
  and the periods are right.
- **WhatsApp as a channel:** files pass through Meta's servers, encrypted end to end, and sit on
  the founder's phone and computer until deleted. The legal notice says so (same section). Check
  that the privacy page lists WhatsApp
  among the ways we receive data (CHW-359).
- **14 days for a stalled setup:** check that it is short enough.
