# DM outreach: scripts, list and tracker

Written 2026-11-03 for CHW-347, from `2026-11-messaging.md`. That page decides what may be
claimed; this one decides how it is said in a DM. The founder sends every message by hand, from
their own account, in the language the coach posts in: French, Spanish or English.

Fill the brackets before sending: `[prénom]`, `[sujet]`, `[ce que tu en as retenu]`,
`[WhatsApp]` (the founder's number, with the country code), and in the notice (§4) `[nom]` and
`[e-mail]`, the founder's.

Write in the coach's `language`; pick the proof in follow-up 2 by their `country`.

## 1. The rules

1. **One true, specific line about one of their posts, or no message.** If you can't write it in
   a minute, skip the coach. A line that would fit any coach reads as a bot.
2. **No link in the first message.** A first DM from a stranger lands in the message requests,
   and a link makes it look like spam. The link comes once they answer.
3. **At most two follow-ups,** on day 3 and day 7, and none once they have answered anything,
   "no" included. Then the row is closed. Instagram lets someone who doesn't follow you send one
   text message request until it is accepted, so a follow-up is only possible once they have
   accepted the request (or follow you back). If Instagram won't let you send it, don't look for
   a way round: leave `followup_1` and `followup_2` empty and the row closes on day 7.
4. **"No" or no interest: answer once, politely, and never write again. "Stop": answer nothing**
   (message 1 promised no more messages). Set `outcome` to `no` or `stop`; the row is cut down
   at the next Monday review (§4), after the week's totals are counted, and what is left is only
   there so they are never written to again.
5. **By hand, never automated.** No tool sends, schedules or copies DMs in bulk: the account
   would be flagged, and it would stop being founder-led.
6. **30 new coaches a day,** rotating countries in the list's proportions: about 15 in Morocco,
   8 in France or Belgium and 7 in Spain, whatever language they post in. Until the consent
   question in §4 is answered, all 30 go to France, and only French coaches go on the list.
   Follow-ups come on top. If Instagram shows a warning or blocks sending, stop for 48 hours,
   then halve the pace for a week.
7. **Only what the messaging doc allows.** No competitor named in an opener (messaging §4), no
   "en 5 minutes" (§6), no client counts and no price in MAD (§7).

## 2. The scripts

### Message 1: the opener and the offer

The personal line, then the core line (their first workout is ready the day they accept), then
proof 4 (done for you) and the Free plan. It ends with a question, so there is something to
answer, and then one line on their details: the short notice (§4), which says what is kept, why,
where the rest is said, and how to say no. "Le jour même" holds because the setup always
includes a starter program (messaging §3); a coach who sets up alone is told to pick one (the
reply to a yes).

- **FR:** Salut [prénom]. Ton post sur [sujet] m'a parlé : [ce que tu en as retenu]. Je fais FitPal Coach, une app où tes abonnés demandent à s'entraîner avec toi depuis le lien de ta bio, et leur première séance est prête le jour où tu acceptes. Si tu m'envoies ton programme, en PDF ou en Sheet, je te le mets sur l'app en 24 h, sans rien payer : c'est gratuit jusqu'à 3 clients. Ça t'intéresse ? (J'ai noté ton @ et ce que montre ton profil public, pour te présenter FitPal Coach, avec deux relances au plus ; le détail est sur le lien de ma bio. Réponds « stop » et je ne t'écris plus.)
- **ES:** Hola [prénom]. Tu post sobre [sujet] me llegó: [ce que tu en as retenu]. Hago FitPal Coach, una app donde tus seguidores piden entrenar contigo desde el enlace de tu bio, y su primer entreno está listo el día que aceptas. Si me mandas tu programa, en PDF o en Sheet, te lo paso a la app en 24 h, sin pagar nada: es gratis hasta 3 clientes. ¿Te interesa? (He apuntado tu @ y lo que muestra tu perfil público, para presentarte FitPal Coach, con dos mensajes más como mucho; los detalles, en el enlace de mi bio. Responde «stop» y no te escribo más.)
- **EN:** Hi [prénom]. Your post about [sujet] stuck with me: [ce que tu en as retenu]. I make FitPal Coach, an app where your followers ask to train with you from the link in your bio, and their first workout is ready the day you accept. If you send me your program, as a PDF or a Sheet, I'll put it in the app within 24 hours, at no cost: it's free up to 3 clients. Interested? (I've noted your handle and what your public profile shows, to tell you about FitPal Coach, with two follow-ups at most; the details are at the link in my bio. Reply "stop" and I won't write again.)

with a code sent to their own e-mail address, and the admin panel can't make one for them.

  Después, como prefieras. Yo me encargo del resto: mándame por WhatsApp ([WhatsApp]) tu programa (PDF, Sheet o fotos), una foto tuya y dos líneas sobre lo que haces, y te devuelvo tu enlace en 24 h. O lo haces tú: en tu página, elige un programa de inicio, es el que está listo el día que aceptas. En los dos casos, avísame cuando tengas la cuenta.
- **EN:** Great. First, make your account, it takes a minute: open this link, then tap the three dots at the top right and "Open in browser" (inside Instagram the account can't be made), or copy it into Safari or Chrome: https://coach.fitpal.ma/#/start?plan=free

Or you do it yourself: on your page, pick a starter program, that's what is ready the day you accept. Either way, tell me when your account is made.

Then send the full notice (§4) as a second message. When they say the account is made, ask
which e-mail they signed up with, search the admin panel's users list by it (a name isn't
unique; an address they haven't confirmed yet shows as "(unconfirmed)" and is found all the same)
and fill `signed_up`. The e-mail itself never goes in the sheet.

Why the browser line: coach sign-up (`Start.jsx`) used to make the account with a passkey, which
fails in Instagram's in-app browser (`lib/inapp.js`). Since CHW-368 it sends a 6-digit code by
e-mail instead, which works there, and offers the passkey afterwards, outside Instagram only.
Keep the line until CHW-368's test on a real iPhone and Android phone inside Instagram passes,
then drop it here and in `2026-11-content-calendar.md` §5.

The setup itself is `done-for-you.md` (CHW-348): what the coach sends, how the founder builds the
program in their own account and copies it into the coach's from the admin panel, the message
that sends the coach their part, and when their files are deleted. Stop offering the 24 hours if
the queue goes past a day.

- **ES:** Último mensaje por mi parte. Tus clientes te siguen pagando como ahora, transferencia, efectivo o CashPlus: FitPal no toca tu dinero. Si algún día te interesa, responde solo «sí».

France, Belgium and Spain:

- **ES:** Último mensaje por mi parte. Un solo precio, todo incluido, sin extras de pago, y es gratis hasta 3 clientes. Si algún día te interesa, responde solo «sí».

### When they say no

- **FR:** Pas de souci, merci d'avoir répondu. Bonne continuation.
- **ES:** Sin problema, gracias por responder. Mucho ánimo.
- **EN:** No problem, thanks for replying. All the best.

Then set `outcome` to `no` and cut the row down (rule 4). Nothing more is sent. A coach who
answers "stop" gets no reply at all: set `outcome` to `stop`.

### Objections

Answer with the lines in `2026-11-messaging.md` §5, in the coach's language, then go back to the
offer in one sentence: "L'offre tient : ton programme sur l'app en 24 h, gratuit." /
"La oferta sigue: tu programa en la app en 24 h, gratis." / "The offer stands: your program in the
app within 24 hours, free." Anything not answered there (a question about a feature, a price, a
competitor): say you'll check, and check before answering. Never guess.

## 3. Building the list

**Who goes on it:**
- an independent coach (not a gym, a brand or a supplement shop) with 5k–100k followers;
- on a professional Instagram account (business or creator: the profile shows a category such
  as "Personal trainer" or "Coach"), so the message goes to their business, not their private life;
- coaching online or one to one;
- posting in French, Spanish or English;
- based in Morocco, France, Belgium or Spain (France only until the consent question in §4 is
  answered: no rows for the others before then).

**Where to look:**
- **Searches and hashtags:** coach sportif, coaching en ligne, entrenador personal, online coach,
  with a city (Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Paris, Lyon, Bruxelles, Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia).
- **Coaches' links:** the accounts a coach's bio link or "comment KEYWORD" post leads to.
- **Instagram's suggestions:** the "suggested for you" row on a coach who fits.

Search by hand and copy what the profile shows. No scraping tool.

**Leave off:**
- accounts that mostly sell products;
- coaches above 100k: a cold DM from a stranger rarely gets read at that size. (The affiliate
  offer, CHW-350, starts at 10k and is for coaches already using FitPal, so it is not a reason
  to leave anyone off.)
- accounts with no post in the last month.

A coach already using an app goes on the list: note which app, and expect the Trainerize objection.

## 4. The tracker

`dm-outreach-tracker.csv` is the header row to import into a Google Sheet (File › Import). The
filled sheet lives in that Sheet, **never in this repo**: it holds people's details, and the repo
is not the place for them.

| Column | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| `handle` | their @, without the @ |
| `country` | MA, FR, BE or ES |
| `language` | fr, es or en: the language they post in, which is the one you write in |
| `followers` | the number on the profile the day you add them |
| `niche` | women, bodybuilding, home, running, other |
| `link_tool` | what their bio link is today: Linktree, Stan, WhatsApp, an app, none |
| `app_used` | the coaching app they mention, if any |
| `post` | the post the opener names, as a link |
| `sent` | date of message 1 (YYYY-MM-DD) |
| `followup_1`, `followup_2` | dates sent, left empty once they reply |
| `replied` | date of their first answer |
| `outcome` | `yes`, `no` (they said no), `stop` (they asked us to stop), `objection` (still talking), or empty (no answer yet, or none after follow-up 2) |
| `call` | date of a call, if there is one |
| `signed_up` | date their coach account exists (the admin panel's users list, matched as in the reply to a yes) |
| `last_contact` | date of their last message to us, updated each time; empty if they never answered |
| `notes` | anything worth remembering, in a few words |

Activation, habit and paid are not tracked per coach here: the admin panel's coach funnel
(CHW-335) counts them by sign-up week, not per coach, and the sheet needs no data from inside
the coach's account (§5).

**The data in it:**
- **What goes in:** only what the coach shows publicly for their business. Nothing personal from
  outside their profile.
- **The clock:** a row's age runs from `last_contact`, or from `sent` if they never answered.
  Our own follow-ups don't restart it.
- **Cutting a row down:** at the Monday review, after the week's totals are counted (§5), a row
  closed with `no` or `stop`, and any row 6 months old, keeps only `handle`, `outcome`, `sent` and
  `last_contact`; everything else is cleared. That is a do-not-contact line and the dates its
  deletion runs from, nothing more.
- **Deleting old rows:** a cut-down row is deleted 3 years after the clock above (the CNIL's
  guideline for prospect data and objection lists; to verify, below).
- **Erasure:** a coach who asks to be erased has everything deleted the same day except their
  handle on a do-not-contact line, and is told so: without it, the next search by hand could
  find them and write to them again. If they want that gone too, delete it.
- **Totals:** a second tab in the Sheet holds the weekly numbers (§5), with no names or handles.
- **Other uses:** the list is for these DMs only. It is never added to a newsletter or shared.

**The notice.** Under GDPR Art. 14, someone whose details we collect without asking them gets,
at first contact, who we are, why we hold their details and on what basis, where they came
from, how long we keep them, and their rights. A DM can't carry all of that, and rule 2 keeps
links out of message 1, so it comes in two layers, and both must reach every coach written to,
including the many who never answer:
- **Short notice:** the bracketed line at the end of message 1 (what is kept, what for, where
  the details are, how to say no).
- **Full notice:** the text below, published where message 1 points: the link in the sending
  account's Instagram bio leads to it. **Nothing is sent before it is up** (to do, below). It is
  also sent as is to any coach who asks what we hold or for their row, and after the reply to a
  yes.

The full notice:

**The legal basis.** This is professional prospecting under legitimate interest: a message about
the coach's own work, sent to their business account, with a way to say no in the first message
and nothing after a no.

**To verify before the first send** (checked against the GDPR and Morocco 09-08 checklists in
lawve-ai/awesome-legal-skills; not legal advice):
- **Blocking for Spain, Belgium and Morocco: does a DM need prior consent?** Legitimate interest
  covers the GDPR side only. The electronic-marketing rules sit on top of it, and they treat an
  unsolicited commercial message to a person differently by country:
  - **Spain:** LSSI art. 21 bans commercial messages by e-mail "u otro medio de comunicación
    electrónica equivalente" without prior request or consent, with no exception for
    professionals.
  - **Morocco:** law 09-08 art. 10 bans direct prospecting by e-mail or a similar technology
    using a natural person's details without their prior consent.
  - **Belgium:** the Code de droit économique (art. XII.13) asks for consent for electronic
    advertising; its business exception covers impersonal company addresses, not an
    individual coach's own account.
  - **France:** a professional may be prospected without consent about their own profession,
    with a way to object in every message (CPCE art. L34-5, CNIL guidance). That is what this
    doc is built on.

  Whether an Instagram DM counts as an electronic message under each of these is the question
  to answer first. Until it is, send in France only; the 30 a day and the follow-ups stay as
  written.
- **To do, blocking: publish the full notice.** A "prospecting" section on the privacy page
  (CHW-359), with the sending account's bio link pointing to it. Then check the two layers are
  enough for Art. 14.
- **The balancing test:** legitimate interest needs one written down (the interest, why a DM is
  necessary for it, why it doesn't override the coach's interests). Not written yet; it goes with
  the CHW-359 work.
- **Morocco:** whether this prospect list needs a CNDP declaration under law 09-08. It goes with
  the CNDP work in CHW-359.
- **Records of processing:** whether FitPal keeps an Art. 30 record of processing. If it does,
  add "coach prospecting" to it: purpose, the columns above, 6 months, the founder only, Google
  as the Sheet's host.
- **Retention:** 6 months for the full row and 3 years for the cut-down one follow the CNIL's
  guideline for prospects; check they fit Spain and Morocco too, and that keeping a
  handle-only do-not-contact line after an erasure request is allowed.
- **Google as host:** the Sheet's data may be stored outside the EU. Check Google Workspace's
  terms cover the transfer (with the processor agreements in CHW-359).
- **Instagram's own rules:** they apply on top of the law. The pace in §1 is there for them, too.

## 5. Monday review

First, before cutting any row down, add the week's numbers to the Totals tab, per language and
per country: message 1s sent, first replies, yeses and sign-ups that week (from `sent`,
`replied`, `outcome` and `signed_up`). Then cut rows down (§4). The rates come from the running
totals, which hold no names:

| Number | From the totals |
|---|---|
| Reply rate | replies ÷ sent |
| Yes rate | yeses ÷ replies |
| Sign-up rate | sign-ups ÷ sent |

Activation, habit and paid come from the admin panel's coach funnel (CHW-335), by sign-up week,
not from the tracker. Two things to know when reading it:
- **It counts every self-serve coach,** not only the ones these DMs brought.
- **It reads each week as it is today, not within 7 days.** `activated` counts the week's
  coaches who have a client today (not disabled) who has ever logged a workout, however long it
  took, and even if that workout came before the client joined them. It drops if the coach
  releases that client or the client is disabled or deleted. It can't give the growth plan's "half within 7
  days" exactly. The closest reading: a week's count at the second Monday after it ends, when
  each of its coaches has had 8 to 14 days, which overstates the 7-day rate. Say so when you
  report it. Habit is read only for coaches 30+ days in (`habitDue`); its target is 60%.

**Revising the scripts:**
- **Change one thing at a time:** the personal line's style, the offer's wording, or the
  follow-up's proof.
- **Judge it on the next 100 sends** in that language, against the 100 before. That is the
  "scripts revised from what the replies show" half of CHW-347's done-when.
- **Write down each change and its result** under a dated heading at the end of this file, so the
  next revision starts from what was tried.
