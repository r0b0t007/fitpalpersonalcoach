# Partnerships with trainer schools and gyms

Written 2026-11-08 for CHW-353 (growth plan §4, weeks 3–10). A school that trains fitness coaches
passes our offer to its students and hosts a free 45-minute module on launching online coaching.
Its new graduates get Pro free for 6 months.

**Why it matters more now.** Cold DMs can't go to coaches in Morocco, Belgium or Spain (the
[LIA](../privacy/2026-11-08-lia-coach-prospecting.md) §4). Morocco is the plan's largest market,
so its schools are the main way in, and they start in week 3. France, Belgium and Spain follow in
week 7.

Not legal advice. The agreement in §6 was checked against the contract-review checklist in
lawve-ai/awesome-legal-skills (contract-review). The contact rules in §4 restate the LIA's
research. The points marked "to verify" are for the owner or counsel.

**Done when** (from the issue): 3 partners have signed, and 1 module has been delivered with its
sign-ups counted.

## 2. The offer

**For the school's graduates:**
- **Pro (30 clients) free for 6 months.** Pro is €39 a month on fitpal.ma. Granted from the admin
  panel (`admin.licence.grant`, plan `pro`) once the graduate has an account. It's a `manual`
  licence with no end date, so:
  - put the revoke date in the founder's calendar, with the account's e-mail, and delete that
    entry once it is revoked;
  - tell the coach a week before it ends. They then drop to Free, because production sets
    `FREE_CAP=3`: they keep every client they have, but take no new one while they have 3 or more.
    Check that `/api/config` still shows `free_cap: 3` before revoking (as in the interview kit,
    `2026-11-interviews.md`).
- **Who counts as a graduate:** a coach who signs up through the partner's link (§3) while the
  agreement runs. We never ask the school for a list of its students, and the school never sends
  one.

**For the school:**
- **The guest module, free** (§5): 45 minutes, on site or by video call, in French or Spanish.
- **A certificate of attendance for the module,** co-branded, if the school wants one. It says
  "attended a 45-minute module", never that it qualifies anyone.
- **Its name on fitpal.ma** as a partner, only if it says so in writing. Without that, we don't
  name it anywhere.

No money changes hands either way.

## 3. How sign-ups are counted

Through the coach referral link that already exists (CHW-350,
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-coach-referrals-design.md`): `https://fitpal.ma/?ref=<handle>`.

- **The partner's link belongs to a coach at the school,** usually the trainer who hosts the
  module and coaches clients themselves. They open a FitPal Coach account and publish their page,
  and their page's handle makes the link. Every coach who signs up through it is recorded with them
  as referrer (`users.referred_by`).
- **They earn what any referrer earns:** a free month for each referred coach whose client logs a
  first workout. For a school trainer this is the reason to share the link instead of the bare
  address.
- **The owner counts sign-ups** in the admin panel's audit log: search the Event column for
  `coach.referral.signup`; the About column names the referrer. The log keeps 90 days, so write the count in the Sheet's
  "Partners" tab at each Monday review. Grant Pro (§2) to each new one the same week.
- **The partner sees counts only,** never names: « Inscrits : n · Actifs : n » on their Account
  page. That card shows only while plans apply and join requests are on in production (join
  requests are off until the CNDP answers, CHW-367); until then, the owner tells the partner the
  count.

**To decide (owner): a school with no trainer who coaches.** The link above needs a coach page.
If a school has no such person, the choices are a coach account and page in the school's name,
which would show « Demander à rejoindre » to the public, or a small feature: partner codes that
count sign-ups without a page. Build the feature only if one of the first three needs it.

## 4. Making contact

**Write to the institution, never to its students or staff one by one.** No cold DMs to trainers
at a school, and no messages to students: the school passes the offer on.

| Country | First contact | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Morocco, France | An e-mail to the school's generic address (contact@, info@), or its contact form. A school that publishes only a WhatsApp line (CSA): **call** it, don't message it. | The prior-consent rules protect individuals (09-08 art. 10, CPCE L34-5); a school's generic address is not a person's. Art. 10 covers e-mail, fax and automated calls, not a call made by a person. *To verify (counsel):* that this holds for a Moroccan sender (LIA §4.1). |
| Belgium | The same | The royal decree of 4 April 2003 exempts impersonal addresses of legal persons (LIA §4). |
| Spain | **A call to the switchboard first.** E-mail only once someone has said yes to receiving it. A school with no phone (Fit Generation, ESHI, ICEN): a **letter** to the address in its aviso legal, asking it to reply if interested. No chat, WhatsApp or form message. | LSSI art. 21 needs prior consent for commercial messages by e-mail "or an equivalent electronic means", and doesn't spare companies (LIA §4). A paper letter is outside it. |

- **One follow-up at most,** 10 days later. A no ends it.
- **The "Partners" tab** of the outreach Sheet, one row per school: name, country, contact route
  used, dates, status, next step. A person's name only when they wrote back to us, as the contact
  for the partnership. Delete the row of a school that declined 12 months after its no, and keep a
  partner's row while the agreement runs and 12 months after.

## 5. The guest module (45 minutes)

**Title:** FR « Lancer son coaching en ligne », ES «Lanzar tu entrenamiento online».

Most of it is useful with any tool. FitPal is shown in the last part only: a school won't host a
sales pitch, and students trust a module that doesn't depend on buying anything.

| Minutes | Part | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | The offer | What a monthly online offer contains, and what coaches charge for it (growth plan §1, with its sources). One-to-one or groups. |
| 10–20 | Delivering | A PDF or a Sheet over WhatsApp against an app: logged sets, check-ins, progress photos, and what a client loses when they lose the PDF. |
| 20–30 | The rules | A client's injuries and health are health data: ask before collecting them, keep them only as long as needed (GDPR, law 09-08). No medical claims. A paid post carries its label (« Collaboration commerciale », «Publi»: the founding coaches doc §4). Writing to strangers by DM or e-mail needs care: in Spain, Belgium and Morocco, consent first. Not legal advice, and said so on the slide. |
| 30–40 | Demo | FitPal Coach: the page, a follower asking to join, the program, the first workout. Until join requests are on in production, show the 60-second demo video (CHW-346) and the coach side live. |
| 40–45 | Questions, and the offer | The partner's link (§3) on the last slide, as a QR code. |

**Materials to make (owner):** the slides in FR and ES, the QR code of the partner's link, and the
certificate template. The founder delivers the module; a school trainer can co-host.

### Review (contract-review checklist, not legal advice)

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Blank fields | The bracketed names, addresses and dates, to fill per school. FitPal's legal entity is taken from the legal notice, *to verify* that it is current. |
